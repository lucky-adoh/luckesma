# Hi, I'm Lucky Adoh 👋

I am a researcher specialising in **energy harvesting, vibration and structural dynamics, sensors, and self-powered systems**. My work combines analytical modelling, numerical simulation, experimental testing, signal processing, and data analysis to investigate energy conversion, sensing, and dynamic engineering systems.

I am currently a postgraduate researcher at the **Institute of Sound and Vibration Research (ISVR), University of Southampton, UK**.

## ⚡ Research

My research focuses on **energy harvesting, sensing, vibration, and electromechanical systems**, with particular interest in converting ambient mechanical energy into useful electrical energy and integrating energy harvesting technologies with low-power sensing and energy-storage systems.

My work spans piezoelectric and electromagnetic transduction, linear and nonlinear dynamic systems, vibration analysis, energy conversion, and self-powered sensing. My broader research interests include **energy storage, batteries, energy materials, smart sensing technologies, structural health monitoring, and sustainable engineering systems**.
